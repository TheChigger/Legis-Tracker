H.B. No. 112
A BILL TO BE ENTITLED
AN ACT
relating to retirement benefits for certain law enforcement
officers who are members of the Teacher Retirement System of Texas,
including the creation of a supplemental retirement program.
BE IT ENACTED BY THE LEGISLATURE OF THE STATE OF TEXAS:
SECTION 1.  Section 821.001, Government Code, is amended by
adding Subdivisions (17-a), (17-b), and (17-c) to read as follows:
(17-a)
"Supplemental program member" means a member of
the retirement system who has, is earning, or is eligible to earn
credit for supplemental program service under Subchapter G, Chapter
823.
(17-b)
"Supplemental program service" means
membership service that qualifies as supplemental program service
under Section 823.551.
(17-c)
"Supplemental program service credit" means
the amount of supplemental program service credited to a member
under Subchapter G, Chapter 823.
SECTION 2.  Section 822.005, Government Code, is amended by
adding Subsection (e) to read as follows:
(e)
For a supplemental program member, the withdrawal of
accumulated contributions under Subsection (a) includes all of the
member's contributions made under Section 825.403.
SECTION 3.  Chapter 823, Government Code, is amended by
adding Subchapter G to read as follows:
SUBCHAPTER G. SUPPLEMENTAL PROGRAM SERVICE CREDIT
Sec.
823.551.
SUPPLEMENTAL PROGRAM SERVICE:
QUALIFICATIONS. Membership service qualifies as supplemental
program service if:
(1)
the membership service was provided in a position
with an employer that required the member to hold an officer license
issued by the Texas Commission on Law Enforcement under Chapter
1701, Occupations Code; and
(2)
the primary duty of the position was the
enforcement of the criminal or juvenile laws of this state.
Sec.
823.552.
SUPPLEMENTAL PROGRAM SERVICE: BASIS OF
CREDIT.
Supplemental program service credit is creditable as
provided by rule of the board of trustees.

to read as follows:
(a)  Except as provided by Subsections (c) and (d)
and
Section 824.2035
, the standard service retirement annuity is an
amount computed on the basis of the member's average annual

SECTION 5.  Subchapter C, Chapter 824, Government Code, is
amended by adding Section 824.2035 to read as follows:
Sec.
824.2035.
ENHANCED SERVICE RETIREMENT ANNUITY FOR
SUPPLEMENTAL PROGRAM MEMBERS; ELIGIBILITY.
(a)
Subject to
Subsections (c) and (d) and notwithstanding Section 824.203,
the
standard service retirement annuity of a supplemental program
member under this section is an amount computed on the basis of the
member's average annual compensation for the five years of
supplemental program service, whether or not consecutive, in which
the member received the highest annual compensation, times 2.8
percent for each year of supplemental program service credit in the
retirement system. With respect to any year of the five years of
supplemental program service that is not supplemental service, the
annuity must be computed by applying the percentage factor required
under Section 824.203 instead of 2.8 percent for that year.
(b)
Notwithstanding Section 824.202 and subject to
Subsections (c) and (d), a supplemental program member is eligible
to retire and receive the annuity described by Subsection (a) if the
member has at least 25 years of supplemental program service
credit, regardless of age.
(c)
The annuity described by Subsection (a) of a
supplemental program member who retires before attaining the age of
57 under the eligibility criteria established by Subsection (b) is
actuarially reduced by five percent for each year of difference
between the member's age at retirement and 57.
The actuarial
reduction described by this subsection is in addition to any other
actuarial reduction required by Subsection (d) or other law, and a
member is not entitled to have the annuity recalculated at age 57.
(d)
A supplemental program member who retires under
Subsection (b) before attaining the age of 50 is entitled only to an
annuity that is actuarially reduced from the annuity available at
the age of 50 to the supplemental program member whose service
credit annuity amount is based on the sum of the member's age and
amount of supplemental program service credit and other service
credit, and is not entitled to have the annuity recalculated at age
57.
(e)
A provision of law that applies to a member eligible to
retire under Section 824.202 or receiving a service retirement
annuity under Section 824.203 applies to the same extent and in the
same manner to a member eligible for or receiving a service
retirement annuity under this section, except to the extent the
provision conflicts with this section. Optional retirement
annuities provided by Section 824.204 are available to a member
eligible to receive a service retirement annuity under this
section.
(f)
Notwithstanding any other provision of this subchapter,
a supplemental program member who is eligible to retire under this
section or Section 824.202 is entitled to whichever computation of
a service retirement annuity under this subchapter provides the
higher annuity.

SECTION 7.  Subchapter D, Chapter 824, Government Code, is
amended by adding Sections 824.3045 and 824.3046 to read as
follows:
Sec.
824.3045.
OCCUPATIONAL DISABILITY RETIREMENT BENEFITS
FOR SUPPLEMENTAL PROGRAM MEMBERS. (a) Except as provided by
Subsection (b), an annuity payable under Section 824.304 for a
disability resulting from a risk to which supplemental program
members are exposed because of the nature of the members' duties is
an amount computed on the basis of the supplemental program
member's average annual compensation, times 2.8 percent.
(b)
A disability retirement annuity described by Subsection
(a) is not reducible because of age and may not be less than 50
percent of the supplemental program member's average annual
compensation regardless of the amount of service credited to the
member.
(c)
If a retiring member or retiree under this section
presents evidence satisfactory to the retirement system that the
person's disability makes the person incapable of substantial
gainful activity solely because of the disability and is considered
a total disability under federal social security law, the
retirement system shall increase the person's disability
retirement annuity to 100 percent of the member's average annual
compensation.
(d)
An annuity increase under Subsection (c) is not payable
before the first month following the month in which the
satisfactory evidence is received by the retirement system under
that subsection.
(e)
For purposes of this section, "average annual
compensation" means:
(1)
a member's average annual compensation for the
three highest years of compensation attributable to supplemental
program service; or
(2)
a member's average annual compensation
attributable to supplemental program service if a member retires
with less than three years of service.
Sec.
824.3046.
INFORMATION ABOUT OCCUPATIONAL DISABILITY
OF SUPPLEMENTAL PROGRAM MEMBER. (a) A supplemental program member
who applies for retirement for a disability under Section 824.3045
shall furnish to the retirement system all information and other
data requested by the retirement system and relating to the
disability.
(b)
The retirement system may require information and other
data relating to a disability retirement application to be
furnished by any officer or employee of the employer with which the
applicant holds a position.
(c)
After receiving information and other data the
retirement system considers necessary, the executive director
shall determine, subject to review by the board of trustees,
whether or not the disability resulted from a risk described by
Section 824.3045(a).

SECTION 17.  (a)  An employer participating in the Teacher
Retirement System of Texas shall begin making deductions and
collecting the contributions for the supplemental program as
provided by Section 825.403(a-1), Government Code, as added by this
Act, based on service that is performed on or after September 1,
2029. The employer shall also begin making the additional
contributions under Section 825.4092(f), Government Code, as added
by this Act, and provide the certification required by Section
825.5155, Government Code, as added by this Act, based on service
that is performed on or after September 1, 2029.
(b)  The board of trustees of the Teacher Retirement System
of Texas shall adopt rules as soon as possible after the effective
date of this Act to implement the changes in law made by this Act,
including rules governing the treatment of a member of the Teacher
Retirement System of Texas whose employment in a position described
by Section 823.551, Government Code, as added by this Act, began
before September 1, 2029.
SECTION 18.  (a)  In this section, "supplemental program
member" and "supplemental program service credit" have the meanings
assigned by Section 821.001, Government Code, as amended by this
Act.
(b)  Subject to the requirements of this section, the changes
in law made by this Act apply only to a member of the Teacher
Retirement System of Texas who, on or after the effective date of
this Act, is a supplemental program member.
(c)  A member described by Subsection (b) of this section and
the member's beneficiaries are not entitled to receive payment of a
benefit under Section 824.2035, 824.3045, or 824.4025, Government
Code, as added by this Act, until September 1, 2029. The Teacher
Retirement System of Texas shall pay the benefit described by this
subsection to a member or beneficiary who accrues entitlement to
the benefit under this subsection between the effective date of
this Act and September 1, 2029, as soon as practicable after
September 1, 2029, but not later than September 1, 2030.  Between
the effective date of this Act and September 1, 2029, the retirement
system may provide a service, disability, or death benefit, as
appropriate, to a member or beneficiary who is otherwise eligible
for that benefit under Chapter 824, Government Code, as that
chapter existed immediately before the effective date of this Act.
If the retirement system provides a benefit under prior law in
accordance with this subsection, on September 1, 2029, the
retirement system shall recompute that benefit under Section
824.2035, 824.3045, or 824.4025, Government Code, as added by this
Act, as applicable, as of the date of the member's retirement or
death, as applicable, and pay to the member or the member's
beneficiaries, in a lump sum, the difference between:
(1)  the sum of the amount of the recomputed benefit
accrued between the effective date of this Act and September 1,
2029; and
(2)  the sum of the amount of the benefit paid to the
member or beneficiary under authority of this subsection between
the effective date of this Act and September 1, 2029.
(d)  Except as provided by Subsection (f) of this section,
service credit earned by a member described by Subsection (b) of
this section before September 1, 2029, qualifies as supplemental
program service credit only if:
(1)  the member verifies in accordance with Subsection
(e) of this section that the service credit qualifies as
supplemental program service credit under Section 823.551,
Government Code, as added by this Act; and
(2)  subject to Subsection (e) of this section, the
board of trustees of the Teacher Retirement System of Texas
determines the prior supplemental program service credit can be
established in the retirement system without increasing the period
required to amortize the unfunded actuarial liabilities of the
retirement system to a period that exceeds 30 years by one or more
years.
(e)  As soon as practicable after the effective date of this
Act, the board of trustees of the Teacher Retirement System of Texas
shall determine whether the legislature appropriated sufficient
money to the retirement system for the retirement system to provide
prior supplemental program service credit in accordance with
Subsection (d)(2) of this section.  If the board determines that
insufficient money was appropriated by the legislature to the
retirement system to provide prior supplemental program service
credit in accordance with Subsection (d)(2) of this section, the
board shall adopt rules governing how a supplemental program member
may establish supplemental program service credit for service
performed before September 1, 2029, by verifying that service and
depositing with the retirement system, for each year of
supplemental program service credit, the actuarial present value,
at the time of deposit, of the additional standard retirement
annuity benefits that would be attributable to the purchase of
service credit under this section, based on rates and tables
recommended by the retirement system's actuary and adopted by the
board. The rules must require that the verification of prior
service as supplemental program service credit be completed not
later than September 1, 2030.
(f)  If a member described by Subsection (b) of this section
fails to establish prior supplemental program service credit in
accordance with this section before September 1, 2029, the member
is not entitled to establish the supplemental program service
credit or receive the related benefits authorized by this Act.
SECTION 19.  This Act takes effect September 1, 2027.
