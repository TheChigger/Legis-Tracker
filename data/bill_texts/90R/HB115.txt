H.B. No. 115
A BILL TO BE ENTITLED
AN ACT
relating to managed care plan coverage of a health care service that
was referred by certain primary care providers; creating an
administrative penalty.
BE IT ENACTED BY THE LEGISLATURE OF THE STATE OF TEXAS:
SECTION 1.  Subtitle C, Title 8, Insurance Code, is amended
by adding Chapter 1276 to read as follows:
CHAPTER 1276. HEALTH CARE SERVICES REQUIRING REFERRAL
Sec. 1276.001.  DEFINITIONS. In this chapter:
(1)
"Advanced practice registered nurse" means an
individual licensed by the Texas Board of Nursing as a registered
nurse and recognized by that board as an advanced practice
registered nurse.
(2)
"Direct patient care" and "direct patient care
agreement" have the meanings assigned by Section 117.001,
Occupations Code.
(3)
"Health benefit plan" means a plan that provides
benefits for medical or surgical expenses incurred as a result of a
health condition, accident, or sickness, including an individual,
group, blanket, or franchise insurance policy.
(4)
"Health care service" has the meaning assigned by
Section 117.001, Occupations Code.
(5)
"Managed care plan" means a health benefit plan
under which health care services or supplies are provided to
enrollees through contracts with health care providers and that
requires enrollees to use contracting providers or that provides a
different level of coverage for enrollees who use contracting
providers.
(6)
"Physician" and "physician assistant" have the
meanings assigned by Section 1451.001.
(7)
"Primary care provider" means a physician,
advanced practice registered nurse, or physician assistant who is
trained to serve as the first point of contact with a patient and to
provide continuous comprehensive health care services to a patient.
The term includes a physician who practices family medicine,
general practice, internal medicine, obstetrics, gynecology, or
pediatrics.
Sec.
1276.002.
APPLICABILITY OF CHAPTER. (a) This chapter
applies to a managed care plan issued by a health maintenance
organization operating under Chapter 843 or an insurer offering a
preferred provider benefit plan under Chapter 1301.
(b)  Notwithstanding any other law, this chapter applies to:
(1)  a basic coverage plan under Chapter 1551;
(2)  a basic plan under Chapter 1575;
(3)
a primary care coverage plan under Chapter 1579;
and
(4)
a plan providing basic coverage under Chapter
1601.
Sec. 1276.003.  EXCLUSIONS. This chapter does not apply to:
(1)
a managed care plan that provides coverage only
for:
(A)  a specified disease;
(B)  accidental death or dismemberment; or
(C)
lost wages for a period during which an
employee is absent from work due to sickness or injury;
(2)  credit insurance;
(3)  dental or vision benefits;
(4)
benefits for long-term care, home health care,
community-based care, or benefits for a combination of the types of
care described by this subdivision; or
(5)
a Medicare supplemental policy as defined by
Section 1882(g)(1), Social Security Act (42 U.S.C. Section
1395ss(g)(1)).
Sec.
1276.004.
PROHIBITED PRACTICES RELATED TO REFERRAL BY
PRIMARY CARE PROVIDER. (a) A managed care plan issuer or
administrator may not, solely on the basis that the referral for a
covered health care service was made by an out-of-network primary
care provider:
(1)  deny coverage;
(2)
apply a deductible, coinsurance, copayment, or
other cost-sharing requirement that exceeds the deductible,
coinsurance, copayment, or other cost-sharing requirement
applicable to the service if the referral had been made by a
participating provider;
(3)  assess a facility or administrative fee; or
(4)  assess any other penalty.
(b)
A managed care plan issuer or administrator may not
refuse to recognize a referral for a covered health care service on
the basis that the referral was made by a primary care provider
that:
(1)  is an out-of-network provider;
(2)
provides direct patient care under a direct
patient care agreement; or
(3)
is not credentialed by the issuer or administrator
if the provider is authorized to provide primary care services in
this state and is acting within the scope of the provider's license,
certificate, or other authorization.
Sec.
1276.005.
ENFORCEMENT. (a) In addition to other
enforcement authority under Subtitle B, Title 2, the commissioner
may impose an additional administrative penalty in an amount not to
exceed $5,000 against a managed care plan issuer or administrator
that violates this chapter. The commissioner shall impose the
additional administrative penalty in accordance with Chapter 84.
(b)
A violation of Section 1276.004(a)(3) or (4) is an
unfair method of competition or an unfair or deceptive act or
practice in the business of insurance under Chapter 541 and is
subject to enforcement under that chapter.
(c)  For purposes of enforcing this chapter:
(1)
each denial of coverage, application of an
additional deductible, coinsurance, copayment, or other
cost-sharing requirement, or assessment of an additional facility
or administrative fee or other penalty in violation of this chapter
is considered a separate violation; and
(2)
each day a violation described by Subdivision (1)
continues is considered a separate violation.
Sec.
1276.006.
RULES. The commissioner may adopt rules
necessary to implement this chapter.
SECTION 2.  The changes in law made by this Act apply only to
a managed care plan delivered, issued for delivery, or renewed on or
after the effective date of this Act.
SECTION 3.  This Act takes effect September 1, 2027.
