SECTION 2.  Section 50.0011, Parks and Wildlife Code, is
amended to read as follows:

[

(1)  an individual who has resided continuously in this

SECTION 3.  Chapter 50, Parks and Wildlife Code, is amended
by adding Section 50.006 to read as follows:
Sec.
50.006.
FEE WAIVERS FOR SUPER COMBINATION HUNTING
LICENSE AND ALL-WATER FISHING PASS. (a) In this section:
(1)
"Qualified veteran with a disability" has the
meaning assigned by Section 42.012.
(2)
"Resident" means an individual who has resided
continuously in this state for more than six months immediately
before applying for a super combination hunting license and
all-water fishing pass under this section.
(b)
The commission shall waive the fee for the issuance of a
super combination hunting license and all-water fishing pass to:
(1)
a qualified veteran with a disability, regardless
of whether the individual is a resident;
(2)
a resident who is an active duty member of the
United States armed services or the Texas military forces, as
defined by Section 437.001, Government Code; and
(3)
a resident who is an honorably retired peace
officer, as defined by Section 614.121, Government Code.
(c)
A super combination hunting license and all-water
fishing pass consists of:
(1)  a resident hunting license;
(2)  a migratory game bird stamp;
(3)  an upland game bird stamp;
(4)  an archery stamp;
(5)  a resident fishing license;
(6)  a freshwater fish stamp;
(7)  a saltwater sportfishing stamp;
(8)  a spotted seatrout tag; and
(9)  a red drum tag.
SECTION 4.  (a)  Section 50.006, Parks and Wildlife Code, as
added by this Act, applies only to a fee charged for a license,
stamp, or tag described by Subsection (c) of that section that is
issued on or after the effective date of this Act.
(b)  The Parks and Wildlife Department is not required to
issue a refund for a license, stamp, or tag described by Section
50.006(c), Parks and Wildlife Code, as added by this Act, that is
issued before the effective date of this Act.
SECTION 5.  This Act takes effect September 1, 2027.
