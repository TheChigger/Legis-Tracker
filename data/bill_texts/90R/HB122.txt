H.B. No. 122
A BILL TO BE ENTITLED
AN ACT
relating to the definition of emergency vehicle for the purposes of
weight limitations.
BE IT ENACTED BY THE LEGISLATURE OF THE STATE OF TEXAS:
SECTION 1.  Section 622.952(c), Transportation Code, is
amended to read as follows:
(c)  In this section, "emergency vehicle" means a vehicle
designed to be used under emergency conditions:
(1)  to transport personnel and equipment; [
and
]
(2)  to support the suppression of fires and mitigation
of other hazardous situations
; or
(3)
by an electric utility, as defined by Section
31.002, Utilities Code
.
SECTION 2.  This Act takes effect September 1, 2027.
