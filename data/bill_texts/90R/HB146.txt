SECTION 3.  Subchapter D, Chapter 2303, Occupations Code, is
amended by adding Sections 2303.1553 and 2303.1554 to read as
follows:
Sec.
2303.1553.
ONE-TIME ADJUSTMENT TO CERTAIN FEES:
TEMPORARY PROVISION.
(a)
Not later than February 1, 2028, the
commission shall:
(1)
adjust the impoundment fee under Section
2303.155(b)(2), the storage fees under Section 2303.155(b)(3), and
the maximum amount of the notification fee under Section
2303.155(c) by an amount equal to the amount of the applicable fee
or maximum amount in effect on December 31, 2005, multiplied by the
percentage increase or decrease, if any, in the consumer price
index, as defined by Section 2303.1552(a), that occurred during the
period beginning December 31, 2005, and ending December 31, 2019;
and
(2)
if a fee or maximum amount is adjusted under
Subdivision (1), publish the adjusted fee or maximum amount on the
department's Internet website.
(b)  This section expires September 1, 2029.
Sec.
2303.1554.
ONE-TIME INCREASE TO NOTIFICATION FEE
MAXIMUM AMOUNT: TEMPORARY PROVISION. (a) Not later than February 1,
2028, the commission shall increase the maximum amount of the
notification fee under Section 2303.155(c) by an amount equal to
the sum of:
(1)
the maximum amount of the notification fee in
effect on December 31, 2019, multiplied by the percentage increase
the commission applied to the impoundment fee under Section
2303.155(b)(2) and the storage fees under Section 2303.155(b)(3) in
the final rule adopted by the commission under Section 2303.1552(b)
on October 25, 2019, 44 Tex. Reg. 6365 (2019);
(2)
the maximum amount of the notification fee as
adjusted under Section 2303.1553(a)(1) multiplied by the
percentage increase the commission applied to the impoundment fee
under Section 2303.155(b)(2) and the storage fees under Section
2303.155(b)(3) in the final rule adopted by the commission under
Section 2303.1552(b) on October 29, 2021, 46 Tex. Reg. 7399 (2021);
and
(3)
the maximum amount of the notification fee as
adjusted under Subdivision (2) multiplied by the percentage
increase the commission applied to the impoundment fee under
Section 2303.155(b)(2) and the storage fees under Section
2303.155(b)(3) in the final rule adopted by the commission under
Section 2303.1552(b) on January 5, 2024, 49 Tex. Reg. 38 (2024).
(b)  This section expires September 1, 2029.
SECTION 4.  This Act takes effect September 1, 2027.
