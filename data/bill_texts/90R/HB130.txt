H.B. No. 130
A BILL TO BE ENTITLED
AN ACT
relating to a grant program using money from the fund for veterans'
assistance to support and expand veterans treatment court programs
in this state.
BE IT ENACTED BY THE LEGISLATURE OF THE STATE OF TEXAS:
SECTION 1.  Sections 434.017(c) and (d), Government Code,
are amended to read as follows:
(c)  Money in the fund may only be appropriated to the Texas
Veterans Commission.  Money appropriated under this subsection
shall be used to:
(1)  make grants to address veterans' needs;
(2)  make grants to provide pro bono legal services to
veterans, active duty members of the United States armed forces,
and members of the state military forces;
(3)  administer the fund; [
and
]
(4)  analyze and investigate data received from the
federal Public Assistance Reporting Information System (PARIS)
that is administered by the Administration for Children and
Families of the United States Department of Health and Human
Services
; and
(5)
award grants under Subchapter L to support and
expand veterans treatment court programs in this state
.
(d)  The Texas Veterans Commission may adopt rules governing
the award of grants by the commission under this section
and
Subchapter L
.  The commission shall adopt rules governing the award
of grants to veterans county service offices under Subsection
(c-3).
SECTION 2.  Chapter 434, Government Code, is amended by
adding Subchapter L to read as follows:
SUBCHAPTER L. GRANT PROGRAM TO SUPPORT AND EXPAND VETERANS
TREATMENT COURT PROGRAMS
Sec. 434.501.  DEFINITIONS. In this subchapter:
(1)  "Commission" means the Texas Veterans Commission.
(2)
"Grant program" means the grant program
established under this subchapter.
(3)
"Veterans treatment court program" means a program
established under Chapter 124 or former law.
Sec.
434.502.
GRANT PROGRAM. The commission shall
establish a grant program to support and expand veterans treatment
court programs operated in this state. The commission shall award
grants under the program using money appropriated to the commission
under Section 434.017(c).
Sec.
434.503.
APPLICATION. (a) A county that has
established a veterans treatment court program or two or more
counties that have elected to establish a regional veterans
treatment court program may apply to the commission for funds under
the grant program to support and expand the veterans treatment
court program.
(b)
The application must disclose the other sources of
funding for the veterans treatment court program described by
Subsection (a).
Sec.
434.504.
CRITERIA FOR GRANTS. (a) The commission
shall adopt rules establishing criteria for grant eligibility.
(b)
The commission shall give priority to an applicant that
can demonstrate that there is specific demand for the applicant's
veterans treatment court program, including by showing that the
program has not been able to accept a significant number of
participants into the program.
Sec.
434.505.
USE OF FUNDS. A grant awarded under this
subchapter must be used to support and expand the operation of a
veterans treatment court program and to improve the outcomes of
participants in that expanded program. The capacity of the program
to accept participants may be increased by adding case managers,
community supervision personnel, and peer support or treatment
coordination personnel to the program.
Sec.
434.506.
REPORT BY GRANT RECIPIENT. A grant recipient
shall annually submit to the commission a report detailing the use
of the funds and providing specific information sufficient to
determine whether the funds increased the capacity of the
applicable veterans treatment court program to accept participants
into the program and improved outcomes for those participants. The
report must include:
(1)
the number of referrals to the program, admissions
to the program, and individuals unable to enter the program due to
capacity limitations;
(2)
the number of individuals who successfully
completed the program;
(3)  the caseloads of program personnel; and
(4)
any other outcome measures established by the
commission.
Sec.
434.507.
BIENNIAL REPORT. Not later than December 1 of
each even-numbered year, the commission shall submit a report to
the legislature regarding the results of the grant program
administered under this subchapter.
SECTION 3.  This Act takes effect September 1, 2027.
