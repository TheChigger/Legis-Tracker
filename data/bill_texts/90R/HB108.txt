H.B. No. 108
A BILL TO BE ENTITLED
AN ACT
relating to a study by the Public Utility Commission of Texas on the
effects of certain electric utility monopolies.
BE IT ENACTED BY THE LEGISLATURE OF THE STATE OF TEXAS:
SECTION 1.  (a)  In this Act, "commission" means the Public
Utility Commission of Texas.
(b)  The commission shall conduct a study to evaluate the
effects of a monopoly held by each electric utility that is the sole
provider of electrical generation, transmission, and distribution
service to over 450,000 customers located in a service area that:
(1)  is located outside the ERCOT power region;
(2)  includes at least 10,000 square miles; and
(3)  includes a municipality with a population of more
than 500,000.
(c)  The evaluation must include the effects of each monopoly
described by Subsection (b) of this section on ratepayers and
energy markets in the region.
(d)  The study must include recommendations for
incorporating customer choice, as defined by Section 31.002,
Utilities Code, into each service area affected by a monopoly
described by Subsection (b) of this section.
(e)  The commission shall submit a report of the results of
the study completed under Subsection (b) of this section and the
recommendations required by Subsection (d) of this section to the
legislature not later than December 31, 2028.
(f)  This Act expires January 1, 2029.
SECTION 2.  This Act takes effect September 1, 2027.
