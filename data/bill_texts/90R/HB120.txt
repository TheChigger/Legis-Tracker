H.B. No. 120
A BILL TO BE ENTITLED
AN ACT
relating to transitional support services and housing resources for
foster youth transitioning to independent living.
BE IT ENACTED BY THE LEGISLATURE OF THE STATE OF TEXAS:
SECTION 1.  Section 264.121, Family Code, is amended by
adding Subsection (a-8) and amending Subsections (i) and (j) to
read as follows:
(a-8)
The department shall ensure that all youth enrolled in
the Preparation for Adult Living Program are provided with
comprehensive information regarding available housing resources
and assistance for transitioning youth, including the federal
Foster Youth to Independence initiative administered by the United
States Department of Housing and Urban Development, to improve
awareness of housing assistance options as each youth prepares for
independent living.
(i)  The department shall ensure that the transition plan for
each youth 16 years of age or older includes provisions to assist
the youth in managing the youth's housing needs after the youth
leaves foster care, including provisions that:
(1)  identify the cost of housing in relation to the
youth's sources of income, including any benefits or rental
assistance available to the youth;
(2)  if the youth's housing goals include residing with
family or friends, state that the department has addressed the
following with the youth:
(A)  the length of time the youth expects to stay
in the housing arrangement;
(B)  expectations for the youth regarding paying
rent and meeting other household obligations;
(C)  the youth's psychological and emotional
needs, as applicable; and
(D)  any potential conflicts with other household
members, or any difficulties connected to the type of housing the
youth is seeking, that may arise based on the youth's psychological
and emotional needs;
(3)  inform the youth about emergency shelters and
housing resources, including supervised independent living
, the
federal Foster Youth to Independence initiative administered by the
United States Department of Housing and Urban Development,
and
housing at colleges and universities, such as dormitories;
(4)  require the department to review a common rental
application and a common rental contract with the youth and ensure
that the youth possesses all of the documentation required to
obtain rental housing; and
(5)  identify any individuals who are able to serve as
cosigners or references on the youth's applications for housing.
(j)  The department shall ensure that the transition plan for
each youth 16 years of age or older includes information about
securing or transferring governmental assistance the youth may
qualify for, including:
(1)  social security benefits;
(2)  veteran or service member benefits;
(3)  supplemental nutrition assistance;
(4)  special supplemental nutrition assistance for
women, infants, and children;
(5)  temporary assistance for needy families; and
(6)  housing assistance
, including the federal Foster
Youth to Independence initiative administered by the United States
Department of Housing and Urban Development
.
SECTION 2.  Subchapter B, Chapter 264, Family Code, is
amended by adding Sections 264.1216, 264.1217, and 264.1218 to read
as follows:
Sec.
264.1216.
SUPPORTIVE SERVICES FOR CERTAIN YOUNG
ADULTS.
(a)
The department shall provide ongoing transitional
living services and other supportive services to an eligible young
adult who has exited foster care but continues to face housing
instability or other challenges in achieving self-sufficiency.
Services provided under this section may include:
(1)
services available under the department's
Transitional Living Services Program described by Section 264.121;
(2)  case management;
(3)  housing navigation or assistance;
(4)  life-skills training; and
(5)
other appropriate services to facilitate a young
adult's successful transition to independent living.
(b)
Notwithstanding other law, a young adult is eligible for
services under this section if the young adult:
(1)
is at least 18 years of age but younger than 25
years of age, and was in the conservatorship of the department on
the day preceding:
(A)  the young adult's 18th birthday; or
(B)
the date the young adult aged out of foster
care; and
(2)  meets at least one of the following criteria:
(A)
the young adult is homeless or is at imminent
risk of becoming homeless; or
(B)
the young adult has been issued or assigned a
housing voucher under the federal Foster Youth to Independence
initiative administered by the United States Department of Housing
and Urban Development.
(c)
Notwithstanding Subsection (b) or any other law, the
department may continue to provide transitional living and
supportive services under this section to a young adult after the
young adult's 25th birthday for as long as the young adult is
receiving a housing voucher under the federal Foster Youth to
Independence initiative to ensure stability for the young adult and
young adult's successful completion of the housing voucher program.
(d)
The commissioner may adopt rules to implement this
section, including rules:
(1)
providing for coordination between relevant state
agencies and community partners; and
(2)
establishing any additional eligibility criteria
or service guidelines necessary to maximize the use of available
federal funds and other resources.
Sec.
264.1217.
HOUSING TRANSITION PLANNING.
(a)
For each
youth in the conservatorship of the department who is expected to
exit foster care on or after the youth's 18th birthday, the youth's
caseworker shall, not later than the first day of the sixth month
before the month of the youth's 18th birthday:
(1)
assess the youth's housing needs and preferences
for the period after the youth leaves foster care, including
whether the youth intends to voluntarily enter extended foster care
or will leave foster care on the youth's 18th birthday; and
(2)
identify and initiate engagement with specific
potential housing resources available to the youth after the youth
leaves foster care, which may include placement in extended foster
care, a supervised independent living setting, a transitional
housing program, a federal or state housing assistance program, a
supportive housing service, or another housing arrangement
appropriate for the youth.
(b)
For each youth in the conservatorship of the department
who is expected to leave foster care on or after the youth's 18th
birthday, the youth's caseworker shall, not later than the 90th day
before the youth's 18th birthday:
(1)
complete any necessary referral or application for
housing assistance or housing programs the youth may qualify for,
including an application for a housing voucher under the federal
Foster Youth to Independence initiative administered by the United
States Department of Housing and Urban Development;
(2)
review and confirm the status of the youth's
planned housing arrangements and resources identified under
Subsection (a);
(3)
address any outstanding requirements or barriers
to securing the planned housing arrangements identified under
Subsection (a); and
(4)
ensure that the youth's transition plan is updated
as necessary to reflect a specific, viable housing option for the
youth when the youth leaves foster care.
Sec.
264.1218.
FOSTER YOUTH TO INDEPENDENCE HOUSING VOUCHER
PROGRAM TRACKING SYSTEM.
(a)
In this section, "program" means the
housing voucher program provided by the federal Foster Youth to
Independence initiative administered by the United States
Department of Housing and Urban Development.
(b)
The department shall develop and implement a
centralized, statewide information system to collect and track data
on the demand for, allocation of, and use of the program by former
foster youth in this state. The system must be designed to
identify:
(1)
the number of current and former foster youth in
each region of the state who request or are identified as in need of
a housing voucher under the program;
(2)
the availability and distribution of housing
vouchers under the program across this state, including the number
of housing vouchers allocated, issued, or pending in each region of
the state; and
(3)
outcomes for youth who receive a housing voucher
under the program, including the rate at which recipients
successfully lease housing using a voucher, the average length of
time youth use the vouchers, and any other housing stability
metrics determined by the department.
(c)
In developing and operating the tracking system under
this section, the department shall coordinate with the Texas
Department of Housing and Community Affairs, local public housing
authorities that participate in the program, and other relevant
state or local agencies or partner organizations to facilitate the
secure exchange of information necessary to track the demand for
and use of housing vouchers under the program. To the extent
feasible, the system may build upon or integrate with existing data
systems used by the department or another agency with which the
department coordinates.
(d)
The department shall use information from the tracking
system developed under this section to improve planning and
delivery of housing-related services to current and former foster
youth. The department shall also make summary data from the system
available to the Texas Department of Housing and Community Affairs
and other relevant stakeholders to inform interagency efforts to
address the housing needs of foster youth transitioning to
independence.
(e)
The commissioner may adopt rules necessary to implement
this section, including rules that:
(1)  govern interagency data sharing agreements;
(2)
define relevant performance measures or data
elements; and
(3)
ensure appropriate privacy protections for
participating youth.
SECTION 3.  (a)  As soon as practicable after the effective
date of this Act, the commissioner of the Department of Family and
Protective Services shall adopt rules necessary to implement the
changes in law made by this Act.
(b)  Not later than January 1, 2028, the Department of Family
and Protective Services shall fully implement the initiatives
required by this Act, including the provision of extended
supportive services provided under Section 264.1216, Family Code,
as added by this Act, the transition planning improvements under
Section 264.1217, Family Code, as added by this Act, and the Foster
Youth to Independence housing voucher program tracking system under
Section 264.1218, Family Code, as added by this Act.
SECTION 4.  This Act takes effect September 1, 2027.
