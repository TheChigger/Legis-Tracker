H.B. No. 158
A BILL TO BE ENTITLED
AN ACT
relating to minimum standards for county jails relating to pregnant
and postpartum prisoners.
BE IT ENACTED BY THE LEGISLATURE OF THE STATE OF TEXAS:
SECTION 1.  Chapter 511, Government Code, is amended by
adding Section 511.01055 to read as follows:
Sec.
511.01055.
MINIMUM STANDARDS FOR PREGNANT AND
POSTPARTUM PRISONERS. (a) The commission shall adopt reasonable
rules and procedures that:
(1)
establish minimum standards for county jails
relating to conditions of confinement, clothing, nutrition, and
rest for pregnant and postpartum prisoners, including standards
addressing:
(A)
access to bathrooms, maternity hygiene
products, and prenatal mattresses;
(B)  jail cleanliness; and
(C)  jail temperature;
(2)
require each county jail to report to the
commission, in the manner prescribed by the commission:
(A)
the number of pregnant and postpartum
prisoners confined in the jail and the number of those prisoners
confined in administrative segregation;
(B)
the health, mental health, or general support
programs offered by the jail to pregnant or postpartum prisoners;
(C)
the nutritional supplementation offered by
the jail to pregnant or postpartum prisoners; and
(D)
any other information relating to pregnant
and postpartum prisoners the commission determines appropriate;
and
(3)
require external audits of each county jail every
six months to determine compliance with the standards related to
the care provided to pregnant and postpartum prisoners.
(b)
The standards adopted under Subsection (a)(1) must be
based on evidence-based best practices.
(c)
In implementing the external audit requirement adopted
under Subsection (a)(3), the commission may select and contract
with the office of the independent auditor established under
Section 493.0052, in conjunction with the office of the independent
ombudsman of the Texas Department of Criminal Justice, or another
external auditor to conduct the audits.
(d)  The commission shall inspect county jails to:
(1)
determine compliance with the standards adopted
under Subsection (a)(1) and any other law relating to pregnant or
postpartum prisoners; and
(2)
verify the information reported by a county jail
under Subsection (a)(2).
(e)
The commission shall submit an annual report to the
legislature on the implementation of and compliance by county jails
with the standards adopted under Subsection (a)(1). The report
must:
(1)
include all data collected or provided to the
commission under this section during the period covered by the
report;
(2)
identify any deficiencies in the care provided to
pregnant and postpartum prisoners; and
(3)
include any policy recommendations relating to the
care of pregnant and postpartum prisoners.
SECTION 2.  As soon as practicable after the effective date
of this Act, the Commission on Jail Standards shall adopt the rules
and procedures required by Section 511.01055, Government Code, as
added by this Act.
SECTION 3.  This Act takes effect September 1, 2027.
