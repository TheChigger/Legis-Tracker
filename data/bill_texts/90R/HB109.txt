H.B. No. 109
A BILL TO BE ENTITLED
AN ACT
relating to prohibiting members and representatives of designated
foreign terrorist organizations from testifying at meetings of
governmental entities.
BE IT ENACTED BY THE LEGISLATURE OF THE STATE OF TEXAS:
SECTION 1.  Subchapter A, Chapter 551, Government Code, is
amended by adding Section 551.008 to read as follows:
Sec.
551.008.
LIMITATION ON PUBLIC TESTIMONY. (a) In this
section, "designated foreign terrorist organization" means an
organization designated as a foreign terrorist organization by the
United States secretary of state as authorized by 8 U.S.C. Section
1189.
(b)
Notwithstanding Section 551.007, a person who is a
member of or represents a designated foreign terrorist organization
may not testify at a meeting of a governmental body.
(c)
Before a person may testify at a meeting of a
governmental body, the person must complete a sworn statement
indicating that the person is not a member of and does not represent
a designated foreign terrorist organization.
SECTION 2.  This Act takes effect September 1, 2027.
