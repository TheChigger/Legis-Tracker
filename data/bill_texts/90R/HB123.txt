H.B. No. 123
A BILL TO BE ENTITLED
AN ACT
relating to the repeal of laws requiring agreements between certain
sheriffs and the United States Immigration and Customs Enforcement
to enforce federal immigration law and providing a grant program to
cover the costs of implementing those agreements.
BE IT ENACTED BY THE LEGISLATURE OF THE STATE OF TEXAS:
SECTION 1.  Chapter 753, Government Code, is repealed.
SECTION 2.  On and after the effective date of this Act, and
notwithstanding Subchapter C, Chapter 752, Government Code, a
sheriff who entered into an agreement as required by Section
753.051, Government Code, as repealed by this Act, may terminate
that agreement as provided under the terms of the agreement, or
otherwise, by providing written notice to the United States
Immigration and Customs Enforcement within a reasonable period
before the stated date of termination in the notice.
SECTION 3.  On and after the effective date of this Act, the
comptroller may not award any grant under the grant program
described by Subchapter C, Chapter 753, Government Code, as
repealed by this Act.  Any grant awarded under that subchapter
before the effective date of this Act continues to be subject to the
requirements of that subchapter, including any rules or procedures
adopted by the comptroller, related to the administration of the
grant program, and that law is continued in effect for that purpose.
SECTION 4.  An action brought by the attorney general to
enforce Chapter 753, Government Code, as repealed by this Act, that
is pending on the effective date of this Act is dismissed on that
date. Any equitable relief granted under Chapter 753, Government
Code, as repealed by this Act, for purposes of enforcing that
chapter is void.
SECTION 5.  This Act takes effect immediately if it receives
a vote of two-thirds of all the members elected to each house, as
provided by Section 39, Article III, Texas Constitution.  If this
Act does not receive the vote necessary for immediate effect, this
Act takes effect September 1, 2027.
