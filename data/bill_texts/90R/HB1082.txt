A BILL TO BE ENTITLED

AN ACT

relating to prohibiting the operation of virtual currency kiosks;
creating a criminal offense.
       BE IT ENACTED BY THE LEGISLATURE OF THE STATE OF TEXAS:
       SECTION 1.  Subtitle E, Title 3, Finance Code, is amended by
adding Chapter 161 to read as follows:
CHAPTER 161.  VIRTUAL CURRENCY KIOSKS
       Sec. 161.001.  DEFINITIONS.  In this chapter:
        (1)  "Electronic terminal" has the meaning assigned by
Section 31.002.
        (2)  "Virtual currency" has the meaning assigned by
Section 12.001, Business & Commerce Code.
        (3)  "Virtual currency kiosk" means an electronic
terminal that facilitates the exchange of virtual currency for
money, bank credit, or other virtual currency, including by:
        (A)  connecting directly to a separate virtual
currency exchange that performs the actual virtual currency
transmission; or
        (B)  drawing upon the virtual currency in the
possession of the electronic terminal's operator.
       Sec. 161.002.  OPERATION OF VIRTUAL CURRENCY KIOSK
PROHIBITED.  (a)  A person commits an offense if the person
knowingly:
        (1)  owns or operates a virtual currency kiosk located
in this state; or
        (2)  allows another person to operate or install a
virtual currency kiosk on property located in this state that is
owned, leased, or controlled by the person.
       (b)  An offense under this section is a state jail felony.
       SECTION 2.  This Act takes effect September 1, 2027.
