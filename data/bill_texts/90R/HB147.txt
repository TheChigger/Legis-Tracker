H.B. No. 147
A BILL TO BE ENTITLED
AN ACT
relating to a community health clinic grant program.
BE IT ENACTED BY THE LEGISLATURE OF THE STATE OF TEXAS:
SECTION 1.  Chapter 64, Health and Safety Code, is amended by
designating Section 64.001 as Subchapter A and adding a subchapter
heading to read as follows:
SUBCHAPTER A.
TEACHING HOSPITAL ACCOUNT
SECTION 2.  Chapter 64, Health and Safety Code, is amended by
adding Subchapter B to read as follows:
SUBCHAPTER B.
COMMUNITY HEALTH CLINIC GRANT PROGRAM
Sec. 64.051.  DEFINITIONS. In this subchapter:
(1)
"Charitable clinic"
means a private, nonprofit,
community-based organization that provides health care services to
low-income, underinsured, or uninsured individuals at nominal or no
cost through the use of volunteer health care professionals and
health care professional partnerships.
(2)
"Community health clinic" means a
community-driven, nonprofit health clinic located in a medically
underserved area or serving populations that are medically
underserved.
The term includes:
(A)  a community health center;
(B)
a federally qualified health center, as
defined by 42 U.S.C. Section 1396d(l)(2)(B);
(C)  a rural health clinic; and
(D)  a charitable clinic.
(3)
"Grant program" means the community health clinic
grant program established under this subchapter.
Sec.
64.052.
ESTABLISHMENT AND ADMINISTRATION OF GRANT
PROGRAM. The executive commissioner by rule shall establish and
the commission shall administer a community health clinic grant
program to increase access to health care, improve health outcomes,
reduce health disparities, and achieve health equity in medically
underserved areas of this state by:
(1)
improving the provision of preventative care,
screening, and treatment for chronic health conditions, including
diabetes, hypertension, and obesity; and
(2)
strengthening the availability and capacity of
community health clinics to provide affordable, high-quality
health care services to medically underserved individuals.
Sec.
64.053.
APPLICATION. (a)
A community health clinic
may apply to the commission to receive money under the grant program
if the clinic:
(1)
is located in a county with a population of 800,000
or more, adjacent to a county with a population of 4 million or
more; and
(2)  either:
(A)
operates in a medically underserved area of
the county; or
(B)
serves a medically underserved population in
the county.
(b)
An application submitted under this section must
identify the specific purpose for which the grant is requested,
such as preventative care, screening, or treatment for diabetes,
hypertension, or obesity.
Sec.
64.054.
AWARD OF GRANTS. (a) The executive
commissioner, by rule, shall establish eligibility criteria for the
commission to award grants under the grant program.
(b)
The commission may award a grant under the grant program
to an eligible applicant for the specific purpose identified in the
application under Section 64.053(b).
(c)
The amount of a grant awarded under this section for a
specific purpose identified in the application under Section
64.053(b) may not exceed $5 million.
(d)
A community health clinic may apply for and be awarded
multiple grants, provided each grant is awarded for a specific
purpose identified in the application.
Sec.
64.055.
USE OF GRANT MONEY. A grant recipient may use
money awarded under the grant program only for the specific purpose
identified in the application under Section 64.053(b).
The
executive commissioner shall adopt rules governing the use of money
awarded under the grant program.
Sec.
64.056.
FUNDING. The commission shall award grants as
provided by this subchapter using money appropriated to the
commission for that purpose.
Out of the appropriated money, the
commission shall allocate $200 million each fiscal biennium for
grant awards under this subchapter.
Sec.
64.057.
RULES. The executive commissioner shall adopt
rules necessary to implement and administer this subchapter.
SECTION 3.  Not later than January 1, 2028, the executive
commissioner of the Health and Human Services Commission shall
adopt the rules necessary to implement Subchapter B, Chapter 64,
Health and Safety Code, as added by this Act.
SECTION 4.  This Act takes effect September 1, 2027.
