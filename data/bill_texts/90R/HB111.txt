H.B. No. 111
A BILL TO BE ENTITLED
AN ACT
relating to a prohibition on the installation or construction of
border security infrastructure on state park property.
BE IT ENACTED BY THE LEGISLATURE OF THE STATE OF TEXAS:
SECTION 1.  Chapter 22, Parks and Wildlife Code, is amended
by adding Subchapter A to read as follows:
SUBCHAPTER A. PROVISIONS APPLICABLE TO STATE PARKS GENERALLY
Sec.
22.001.
BORDER SECURITY INFRASTRUCTURE ON STATE PARK
PROPERTY PROHIBITED. (a) In this section:
(1)
"Border security infrastructure" means a wall,
fence, or other physical barrier designed or adapted to impede the
movement of persons or objects crossing the Texas-Mexico border.
(2)
"Governmental entity" means any department,
commission, board, office, authority, or other administrative unit
of this state or of any political subdivision of this state that
exercises governmental functions under the authority of the laws of
this state.
(b)
Notwithstanding any other law, a governmental entity
may not enter into a lease or other agreement for the installation
or construction of border security infrastructure on state park
property.
SECTION 2.  This Act takes effect immediately if it receives
a vote of two-thirds of all the members elected to each house, as
provided by Section 39, Article III, Texas Constitution.  If this
Act does not receive the vote necessary for immediate effect, this
Act takes effect September 1, 2027.
