H.B. No. 129
A BILL TO BE ENTITLED
AN ACT
relating to customer bill payment assistance programs offered by
municipally owned utilities that provide retail water or sewer
utility service.
BE IT ENACTED BY THE LEGISLATURE OF THE STATE OF TEXAS:
SECTION 1.  Subchapter Z, Chapter 552, Local Government
Code, is amended by adding Section 552.9145 to read as follows:
Sec.
552.9145.
CUSTOMER ASSISTANCE PROGRAMS FOR
MUNICIPALLY OWNED WATER OR SEWER UTILITIES. (a) This section
applies only to a municipally owned utility that provides retail
water or sewer utility service.
(b)
Notwithstanding Section 1502.057(b), Government Code, a
municipally owned utility may provide a bill payment assistance
program for retail water or sewer utility service customers:
(1)
who are members of a household with a total annual
income that is at or below 200 percent of the federal poverty
guidelines;
(2)
who are persons with a disability, as defined by 42
U.S.C. Section 12102;
(3)
who are veterans of the United States armed
forces;
(4)  who are 60 years of age or older; or
(5)
whose service was disconnected for nonpayment by
any utility of the municipality three or more times during a
calendar year or who otherwise have a history of service
disconnection for nonpayment, as determined by the municipality.
SECTION 2.  This Act takes effect September 1, 2027.
