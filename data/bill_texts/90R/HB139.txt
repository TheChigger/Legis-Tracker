H.B. No. 139
A BILL TO BE ENTITLED
AN ACT
relating to maximum nonfuel rates certain electric utilities may
charge residential retail customers.
BE IT ENACTED BY THE LEGISLATURE OF THE STATE OF TEXAS:
SECTION 1.  Subchapter E, Chapter 36, Utilities Code, is
amended by adding Section 36.218 to read as follows:
Sec.
36.218.
MAXIMUM NONFUEL RATES CHARGED BY CERTAIN
NON-ERCOT UTILITIES TO RESIDENTIAL RETAIL CUSTOMERS. (a) This
section applies only to an electric utility that operates solely
outside of ERCOT.
(b)
The commission by rule shall adopt a process for setting
during a base rate proceeding a maximum nonfuel rate that an
electric utility may charge residential retail customers.
The rate
must be equal to the average of the nonfuel rates the utility
charged that class of customers at times of summer weather-adjusted
peak demand during the five-year period preceding the year in which
the base rate proceeding occurs, adjusted for inflation.
(c)
Notwithstanding any other provision of this chapter, a
regulatory authority may approve an adjustment of the maximum
nonfuel rate in a proceeding other than a base rate proceeding only:
(1)  for inflation; or
(2)
if the regulatory authority determines that the
rate is unreasonable.
(d)
An electric utility shall provide notice to each
residential retail customer at least 30 days before the date the
utility anticipates charging the customer a new maximum nonfuel
rate set under Subsection (b).
The notice must be provided using
the customer's preferred method of communication with the electric
utility.
SECTION 2.  This Act takes effect September 1, 2027.
